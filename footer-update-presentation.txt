Slide-ready notes comparing the original Figma prototype with this project.

AFTER
Updated local project:

SLIDE 1 - UPDATES AND ANNOUNCEMENTS
- Added an update-section selector for All Updates, Latest News, Announcements, Upcoming Events, and Quick Links.
- Added announcement category navigation for Urgent, Academic, Lab, and Scholarship announcements.
- Category colors are applied consistently to announcement badges, filter chips, and navigation buttons.
- Admins can customize category colors; the choices are saved in the browser for later visits.
- Selecting an update headline focuses and highlights the matching item, with a View All control to restore the full list.
- Selecting a section presents its content on its own and keeps the selected section synchronized with the selector.

SLIDE 2 - DIRECTORY LABEL
- Changed the directory tab label from "Enrollment" to "Population" to better describe the student-population section.

SLIDE 3 - VALIDATION
- Verified the Population label and footer interactions in the browser.
- Verified the footer hover transition and reduced-motion behavior.

FILES UPDATED
- index.html - update and announcement controls, plus footer layout and transition styles.
- app.js - directory tab label.

SLIDE 4 - FOOTER CHANGES
- Panelist feedback called for improved footer alignment and spacing.
- Contact icons now use a consistent 16px alignment slot.
- Footer columns align to the start, with balanced column and row gaps.
- Legal links have a smooth 200ms hover transition with a subtle upward lift.
- Reduced-motion preferences are respected; hover movement is disabled for users who request reduced motion.
